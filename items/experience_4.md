
## Competitive Gaming and Teamwork

**When:** Over several years, including 2026

### What did I do?
I have spent a lot of time practicing competitive video games, especially League of Legends. I have worked on improving my decision-making, learning from matches, and coordinating with teammates in competitive settings.

### What was good about it?
It has helped me think quickly, recognize patterns, and communicate under pressure. Tracking my improvement over time has also been motivating.

### Favourite memory
Becoming friends with my teammates, they are all Waterloo campus students and I haven't found anyone on the Brantford campus who plays the same games as me.

### What didn't I enjoy?
Losses can be frustrating, and competitive games sometimes require more patience than I expect. Team coordination is not always easy, even when everyone wants the same result.

### My grade: A-
The experience taught me about feedback, persistence, and working with people who approach problems differently.
