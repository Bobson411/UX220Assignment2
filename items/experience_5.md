
## Attending Live Concerts

**When:** 2024–2026

### What did I do?
Over the past few years, I have attended several concerts featuring artists I enjoy, including TWICE, BLACKPINK, aespa, and Laufey. I enjoy listening to music, so seeing my favourite artists perform live has been a memorable experience.

### What was good about it?
The best part was experiencing songs I already loved in person. The atmosphere, live performances, and excitement of the crowd made the music feel different from listening to it at home.

### Favourite memory
My favourite memory was attending TWICE's concert. Seeing the members perform live and hearing familiar songs with thousands of other fans made the experience special.

### What didn't I enjoy?
Concert tickets can be extremely expensive, especially when popular artists sell out quickly. Travelling to venues and dealing with large crowds can also be tiring, especially when it is in Toronto and its the same night as a Blue Jay game.

### My grade: A++
Despite the costs, attending concerts has been one of my favourite experiences. I would definitely like to attend more in the future.
