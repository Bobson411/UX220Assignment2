## Working in a Factory

**When:** 2024–2025

### What did I do?
For about nine months, I worked full-time in a factory. My shifts involved long hours, keeping up with production tasks, following workplace procedures, and working with other employees to make sure things got done.

### What was good about it?
The job taught me how to manage responsibilities and stay consistent even when the work was repetitive. Earning my own money also gave me more independence and helped me understand the value of budgeting.

### Favourite memory
My favourite part was seeing how much I could accomplish after sticking with a demanding schedule. Getting through a difficult week felt rewarding because I knew I had earned the results.

### What didn't I enjoy?
The long shifts were exhausting, especially when work continued late into the night. The repetitive tasks could also feel monotonous.

### My grade: B+
It was a good work experience although hard, but it also showed me why I want a career where I can solve creative problems.
