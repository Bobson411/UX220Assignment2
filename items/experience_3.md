
## Learning Graphic Design

**When:** 2025–present

### What did I do?
I have practiced graphic design through university assignments, including creating posters and experimenting with typography, colour, and composition. I have also been learning how to turn rough concepts into finished visual designs.

### What was good about it?
I like having room to experiment. Graphic design has taught me that layout, contrast, and hierarchy can change how someone understands the same information.

### Favourite memory
My favourite part is comparing early drafts with the final result. It is satisfying to see a design become clearer after several rounds of changes and being able to present it in class.

### What didn't I enjoy?
Coming up with enough distinct ideas can be difficult, especially when an assignment calls for many variations. It can also take a lot of time to refine the small details.

### My grade: B+
I want to keep improving both my creativity and my ability to explain design decisions.
