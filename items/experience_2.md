## Program Major Switch


### What did I do?
I switched my academic focus to User Experience Design at Wilfrid Laurier University. I have worked on projects involving user research, information design, prototyping, and communicating design decisions.

### What was good about it?
I enjoy learning how design choices affect real people. It is interesting to connect research findings with practical solutions instead of designing based only on appearances.

### Favourite memory
My favourite moments have been when a group project starts coming together and our ideas turn into something we can actually present or test.

### What didn't I enjoy?
Group coordination and overlapping deadlines can make projects stressful. Sometimes even a small decision takes more discussion than expected. The Waterloo campus is also a lot better than the Brantford campus by x10000.

### My grade: B
This experience is helping me understand what type of design work I want to pursue.
